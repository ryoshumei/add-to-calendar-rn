# Privacy Policy — Add to Calendar

_Last updated: May 24, 2026_

Add to Calendar ("the app") extracts calendar events from text and images you
provide and helps you add them to your calendar. This policy explains what data
the app handles.

## What we collect and why

- **Account email** — If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive
  your email address to create and identify your account. If you use Apple's
  "Hide My Email," we only receive the relay address. Sign-in is optional.
- **Content you submit** — Text and images you provide for event extraction are
  sent to OpenAI for processing, either through our backend (when you are signed
  in) or directly from your device to OpenAI (when you use your own API key). We
  do not store your text or images on our servers after processing.
- **Usage count** — When signed in, we keep a per-account monthly count of
  extraction requests to enforce the free monthly limit.
- **Your OpenAI API key (optional)** — If you add your own OpenAI API key, it is
  stored only in your device's secure Keychain and sent only to OpenAI. It is
  never transmitted to us.

We do **not** use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking, and we do not
sell your data.

## Calendar access

When you add an event, it is written to your device calendar (or opened in
Google Calendar). The app does not read or upload your existing calendar data.

## Third parties

- **OpenAI** — processes the text/images you submit to extract event details.
- **Supabase** — provides authentication and the backend that processes requests
  when you are signed in.
- **Apple / Google** — provide optional sign-in.

## Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time in **Settings → Delete
account**. This removes your account and usage data from our backend and revokes
Sign in with Apple tokens. If you only use your own API key (no sign-in), we
hold no account data about you.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.
